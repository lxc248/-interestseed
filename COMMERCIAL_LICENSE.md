InterestSeed is open source under `AGPL-3.0-only`. That license remains
available to everyone who complies with it.

A separate commercial license is available for cases where AGPL-3.0 is not a
fit, including:

- embedding InterestSeed in a closed-source product;
- offering a hosted or managed version without publishing the corresponding
  source as AGPL-3.0 requires;
- using the InterestSeed name, logo or visual identity in a commercial
  distribution;
- OEM, enterprise or white-label distribution.

## What a commercial license can cover

- a non-copyleft license for the code;
- brand permission for an agreed distribution;
- support and update terms;
- a written scope of permitted use.

Pricing, scope and terms are agreed in writing. Nothing here is a public offer.

## How to ask

Open a GitHub issue with the title prefix `[commercial]` and a short description
of the intended use. Do not include confidential business details in a public
issue; the maintainer will move the conversation to a private channel.

## What does not change

- The AGPL-3.0 license for existing open-source users.
- The trademark policy in `TRADEMARKS.md`.
- The attribution requirements in `NOTICE`.
- The security policy in `SECURITY.md`.
